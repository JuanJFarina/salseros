# SalseRos Vision

## Motivation

Rosario's salsa community discovers social events through scattered Instagram posts. Dates, times, and venues are often embedded in captions or posters, making it unnecessarily difficult to answer a simple question: where can I go dancing this week?

SalseRos combines “salseros” and “Rosario” into a focused local agenda. It should reduce the effort of finding the next social while preserving the informal, welcoming character of the community.

## Goal

SalseRos should become the quickest way to see salsa-related socials happening in Rosario during the next seven days. Events should be presented chronologically, grouped by day, and described with only the information needed to decide where to go.

Visitors should also be able to express lightweight attendance intent through “¡Pa'llá voy!”. This is not a ticket, reservation, or verified guest list. It is a simple community signal that helps dancers see where others plan to gather.

## Product posture

The product should remain:

- Local and Spanish-first.
- Useful without registration or personal information.
- Fast and comfortable on a phone.
- Transparent about event sources and freshness.
- Automated where source information is clear, while surfacing uncertain extraction for human review.
- Easy to operate through a Google spreadsheet instead of a dedicated administration application.

Instagram is an event source, not the product itself. SalseRos should present normalized event information rather than reproduce a social feed.

## What this should not become

The initial product should not become a social network, ticket marketplace, messaging platform, venue-management system, or comprehensive nightlife directory. It should not scrape Instagram, depend on Stories, collect attendee identities, or claim that attendance counts are verified.

It should also avoid publishing uncertain dates or venues merely to appear complete. Missing an ambiguous event is preferable to confidently showing incorrect information.

## North star

A dancer should be able to open SalseRos, understand the coming week's social options in seconds, and indicate “¡Pa'llá voy!” without creating an account.
